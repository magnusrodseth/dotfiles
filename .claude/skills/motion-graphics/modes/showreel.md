# Mode: showreel (engine test, no real subject)

A one-liner tests the engine, never the idea. Use it after setting up a new machine or a new style, then move on: hundreds of people ran the same prompt and the reels rhyme ("brief contagion").

```
make a dynamic 15-second motion graphics video that shows what an incredible motion designer you are,
like it's your showreel for a résumé. go all out.
```
Why it works: "showreel" names a genre with known rules (fast cuts, a new technique every shot, best work first); making the model the subject removes content to get wrong; 15 s is 6-8 shots; "go all out" multiplies effort.

Variants that shipped:
- Style test: "...in the <style> style from styles/<file>.md. Every shot a different technique from that style."
- Story instead of techniques: "use your showreel energy, but tell the history of <topic> from <start> to today, 45 s, 9:16."
- Agency persona: "a 30-second showreel as if you were a niche studio for <audience>. One accent color. Every shot a different technique."

Still run the full pipeline: score first, contact sheet, critique loop.
