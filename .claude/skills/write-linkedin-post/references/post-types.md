# Post archetypes

Five shapes, each derived from posts Magnus actually published. Pick one in Q1,
then follow its structure. Engagement numbers are from the live profile on
28.07.2026 and are there to show what has worked, not as a target.

Full text of every post cited here lives in
[linkedin-posts.md](linkedin-posts.md).

---

## 1. Bygg / ship

**Fires when** he made, shipped, published or open-sourced something. His most
common post and his best-performing one.

**Hook.** Understated, first person, one sentence, ends on `!`. Shrink the thing.

> Jeg har laget et lite verktøy for å snakke med banken min gjennom AI-agenter!

> Etter å ha bygget AI-native applikasjoner i 3 år, har jeg nå pakket alt jeg har lært inn i ett produkt.

**Body.**

1. Qualify the audience honestly, early. "Dette er nok ikke for alle. Men er du
   SpareBank 1-kunde og bruker AI-agenter i hverdagen, er dette kanskje noe for deg."
2. The concrete need that started it, in first person. Not a market gap, a real
   personal friction. "Jeg ville samle hele privatøkonomien min på ett sted."
3. The problem in one paragraph. "Problemet var at agenten ikke kunne se
   banktallene mine."
4. What it does, as an emoji-bulleted list (📊 🧾 💸 🤖) or `→` arrows. One line each.
5. How it works and what is safe about it. Privacy, local, confirmation steps.
6. Plain disclaimer if one is owed. "Dette er et personlig og uoffisielt verktøy."
7. Link line.

**CTA.** Inline `👉` or `👉🏽` line. "👉 Last ned, installer og se koden her: <url>"

**Length.** Standard to deep, 250-400 ord.

**Real examples.** sb1 launch (119 reactions, 13.5k impressions). Eden Stack
launch (157 reactions, 62 comments, 15.8k impressions). Eden Stack Product Hunt
day two, short version (94 reactions, 8.0k).

**Lever worth knowing.** The Eden Stack post pulled 62 comments by asking people
to write a keyword in the comment field for a discount code. It works, it is his
own move, but only use it when he actually has something to hand out.

## 2. Arrangement

**Fires when** he spoke, attended, hosted or won something. Recap or promo.

**Hook.** The feeling first, plainly stated, with the glamour undercut.

> På fredag hadde jeg en av de morsomste faglige dagene jeg kan huske 🕺🧠

> I går fikk jeg stå foran et rom fullt av AI-entusiaster og snakke om noe så glamorøst som ukeshandelen min.

> Jeg og laget mitt kom på 1. plass og vant 30 000 kr i vårt første hackathon! 🕺🏽🤠

**Body.**

1. What he actually presented or built, in two or three sentences. "Kort fortalt:"
   is his compression move here.
2. The interesting mechanic or insight, told so a non-attendee gets value.
3. **The credit block.** Every other named participant, full name, and the
   specific thing they covered. One per line. This is non-negotiable in this
   archetype and it is the reach mechanic.
4. Thanks to the host or organiser by name, with an emoji. "Stor takk til Velo
   Labs for strålende vertskap 🫶🏽", "Takk til Knowit for godt opplegg, gøy
   oppgave og god mat."
5. For a promo, add time and place on its own line. "Tid og sted: 18. mars kl.
   15:00 til 18:00. Torggata 2, 4, 0180 Oslo."

**CTA.** Recap closes on a DM invite ("Send meg en DM, så tar jeg gjerne en
prat!") or a forward-looking question. Promo closes on
"Lenke til påmelding ligger i kommentarfeltet."

**Length.** Standard, 150-300 ord.

**Real examples.** Hackathon win (180 reactions, his highest). CapraCon recap
(103 reactions). Velo Labs housewarming (103 reactions, 8.4k). Capra
AI-fagsamling promo (46 reactions, 9.3k).

## 3. Omtale

**Fires when** someone else published about him. Press, podcast, interview,
feature.

**Hook.** Short, flat, quietly pleased. Never boastful. This archetype is the one
place he writes two-sentence hooks.

> Ukas koder i Kode24. Det er ganske stas.

> Forrige uke delte jeg et lite verktøy som lar AI-agenten min jobbe trygt med mine egne banktall i SpareBank 1. Nå ønsket kode24 at jeg forteller historien om hvordan det ble til. 🕺🏽

**Body.** Two paths.

- **Teaser path.** One paragraph on the range of what he covered
  ("alt fra hvordan vi gir huset ditt en stemme hos Gjensidige, til hvorfor du
  bør ha empati for AI-modellen du bruker"), then a playful invite to go read it.
  Roughly 60 ord total.
- **Story path.** Retell the substance so the post has value on its own, add what
  the coverage tells him about where the industry's attention is
  ("At kode24 ville publisere dette, forteller meg noe om hvor nysgjerrigheten
  ligger nå"), and close on something genuinely fun or self-deprecating.

**CTA.** "Lenke i kommentarfeltet 👇🏽" on the teaser path, inline
"👉🏽 Les saken her: <url>" on the story path.

**Length.** Teaser 50-100 ord, story 250-350 ord.

**Real examples.** Ukas koder teaser (102 reactions). kode24 sb1 story (128
reactions, 15.8k impressions, his best reach).

## 4. Forklaring / nyhet

**Fires when** he read the primary sources so his network does not have to.
Regulation, a model release, a shift in tooling.

**Hook.** The fact, with the stakes and the timing in the same sentence. No
question mark, no drama.

> Fellesferien er over, og den første fristen i EU AI Act som faktisk betyr noe for utviklere, er om under en uke. Samtidig ble den største fristen nettopp skjøvet til desember 2027.

**Body.**

1. What changes and when, concretely. Dates, article numbers, real names.
2. The calming or orienting line, in first person. "Jeg har brukt litt tid på å
   nøste i hva dette faktisk betyr for et utviklingsteam. Kortversjonen er
   roligere enn overskriftene tilsier."
3. **Emoji-bulleted findings**, one emoji per point, chosen to mean something
   (🤖 for tooling, ⚠️ for the risk case, 📋 for the procedural bit, 🇳🇴 for the
   Norway angle). Two to four sentences under each. This is where the value is.
4. Gloss jargon inline the way he does in long-form. "en 'deployer', altså den
   som tar systemet i bruk", "fine-tuning, altså å videre trene en modell på egne
   data".
5. The one thing that actually lands right now, separated out.
6. A genuine question to the reader. "Hvordan står det til hos dere? Jeg er
   nysgjerrig på om dette faktisk er på radaren rundt om i Norge, eller om det er
   en av de tingene som drukner i backlogen."

**CTA.** Inline "👉🏽 Les hele gjennomgangen her: <url>" pointing at the long-form
version.

**Length.** Deep, 350-500 ord. This archetype earns its length.

**Real example.** EU AI Act breakdown (2.1k impressions at one day old).

**Rule.** Never post this archetype on facts that have not been checked against
primary sources. Dates, thresholds and article numbers get verified before the
draft goes out, not after.

## 5. Mening

**Fires when** he has a take he is prepared to defend. Rarest of the five, so
spend it on something he genuinely believes.

**Hook.** State the take flat, with "Ærlig talt" or similar doing the honesty framing.

> Ærlig talt, de fleste AI-demoer du ser om dagen er litt meningsløse.

**Body.**

1. Why the thing he is criticising fails, in one paragraph. "De ser kule ut i en
   kontrollert video, men de overlever sjelden møtet med virkeligheten."
2. What he does instead, with the credibility source named. "Hos oss i Gjensidige
   bygger vi det som faktisk kjører i produksjon."
3. The real texture of the work. Constraints, tradeoffs, what is genuinely hard.
   "Hver dag prøver vi å finne balansen mellom å bevege oss i 100 km/t og det å
   passe inn i det store maskineriet som er Gjensidige."
4. The turn that makes it useful rather than just a complaint. Usually that the
   interesting part is the way of working, not the technology.
5. Where the conversation continues, if there is a talk or a piece attached.

**CTA.** An invitation to disagree, or the event where he will argue it properly.

**Length.** Standard, 200-300 ord.

**Real example.** The "AI-demoer" post leading into the Capra AI-fagsamling (46
reactions, 9.3k impressions).

**Watch out.** This archetype attracts the "det er ikke X, det er Y" cadence,
which is hard-banned. When a contrast wants that shape, write two plain
sentences instead.
